Privacy Policy

Calendar For Life · Calendar For Life Voyages

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

Nothing is collected while you browse. Calendar For Life has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The app asks for an email in exactly two places, both voluntary: when you sign the ship's manifest (which unlocks bookmarks, journals, and galley notes and joins the Festival of the Week letter), and when you send a trip inquiry to Calendar For Life Voyages. If you never do either, no data about you exists.

Data stored on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage to remember:

  • Your bookmarks (voyages saved to My Voyages)
  • Your journal entries (per-voyage personal notes)
  • Your food and drink notes (per-voyage tasting records)
  • Your manifest record (the name and email you signed up with, if you did)
  • A small outbox of pending submissions, kept on your device only until they can be sent, then removed
  • One-time migration flags so the app knows it has applied a one-time data update

Apart from the submissions you explicitly send (below), this data never leaves your device.

Data you choose to share

Two forms in the app send data, and only when you submit them:

  • Signing the manifest sends your email and, if you gave one, your name.
  • A trip inquiry sends your name, email, travel window, party size, your notes, and the festival and port you asked about.

Where it goes

Submissions are delivered through Web3Forms, a form relay service, to the Calendar For Life Voyages inbox. Web3Forms processes the submission solely to deliver it; see the Web3Forms privacy policy for its handling of request data. If a dedicated newsletter provider is added later, this page will name it before it is used. Your data is never sold and never shared beyond that delivery path.

The weekly letter

Signing the manifest joins the Festival of the Week letter: one festival from the almanac, once a week, plus occasional word from Calendar For Life Voyages when trip planning opens for a season. Unsubscribe any time using the link in any letter or by replying with the word unsubscribe. Unsubscribing does not affect the app; bookmarks, journals, and galley notes stay unlocked on your device.

Offline operation

The app installs a Service Worker that caches its own content (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts, icons) so it can work offline. The Service Worker caches only the app itself. It does not cache or transmit any personal data.

Fonts

The app loads the Cinzel and IM Fell English typefaces from Google Fonts on first load. The fonts are then cached locally by the Service Worker. Google's font CDN may log standard web request data (IP address, user agent) per its own privacy policy. The app does not send any personal data to Google.

Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions. It does not access:

  • Your camera or microphone
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your photo library
  • Your files outside the app's own storage
  • Push notifications

Children

The app contains no advertising and no tracking and is safe for users of all ages. The manifest and inquiry forms are intended for adults planning travel. The travel content references restaurants and venues that may serve alcohol; parental discretion is advised for younger readers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be bundled with the next app update and the date above will reflect the change.
